      Getting a usable slope from phone GPS

      1. Phone GPS height is far weaker than position. Expect ±3 to ±15 m vertically, so keep points far apart: about 100 m or more on gentle land.
      2. Record every point with the same averaging time, in open sky, after the fix chip turns green.
      3. Walk the line of steepest slope, from the top point to the bottom point.
      4. When the result says Unreliable, take the RL from an auto level or a DEM and type it into the point. The app then uses that RL for slope.
      5. GPS altitude is height above the WGS84 ellipsoid. That offset cancels out in slope, so do not mix GPS heights with RLs from a benchmark in the same survey.
