GPS slope survey calculator

Enter survey points as latitude, longitude, and elevation. The tool computes horizontal distance (haversine), elevation change, slope in percent and degrees, and cumulative distance along the line. Useful for land grading, irrigation layout, and field topographic checks.

Survey points

One point per line: latitude, longitude, elevation (metres). Decimal degrees. Comma or space separated.

Results

Enter at least two points and click Calculate.

Horizontal distance uses the haversine formula on the WGS84 ellipsoid approximation (mean Earth radius 6 371 km). Elevation is treated as orthometric or ellipsoid height — use consistent vertical datum. Slope % = (rise / run) × 100. This is a field-planning estimate; it is not a substitute for a licensed survey.
Distances are horizontal (plan). Vertical accuracy of consumer GPS is typically several metres; differential or RTK GNSS is preferred for grading work. Cumulative distance is the sum of segment lengths along the survey line.
